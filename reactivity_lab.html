The Reactivity Series

The reactivity series is a list of metals arranged in the order of their decreasing activities. In NCERT Class 10, this is essential for understanding Displacement Reactions and Metallurgy.

The Mnemonic (Memory Trick)

Please Stop Calling Me A Zebra Instead Learn How Copper Mercury Saves Gold

(K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, [H], Cu, Hg, Ag, Au)

Key Rules:

  • Displacement: A metal higher in the series can displace any metal below it from its salt solution.
  • Reaction with Water:
    • K and Na react violently with cold water (catch fire).
    • Mg reacts with hot water.
    • Al, Fe, and Zn react only with steam.
    • Cu, Ag, Au do not react with water at all.
  • Reaction with Acid: Metals above Hydrogen displace it from dilute acids to produce H₂ gas. Metals below (Cu, Ag, Au) show no reaction.
